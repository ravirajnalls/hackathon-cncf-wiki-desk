# Kubernetes

Kubernetes is the graduated CNCF project for running containerized workloads. Pages here cover
its subprojects and features:

- [Agent Sandbox](agent-sandbox.md): the SIG Apps project that adds a Sandbox CRD, templates, claims and warm pools for isolated AI agent workloads.
